# Wellness vs Medical Boundary Guidelines

## Overview
Preventive wellness systems are designed to support lifestyle habits, general fitness routines, and healthy nutritional patterns. They do not operate as clinical diagnostics, medical management tools, or emergency response systems.

## Strict Boundaries: What is Allowed vs Disallowed
1. **Allowed General Wellness Guidance**:
   - Encouraging gradual, realistic physical activity progression.
   - Suggesting balanced whole-food dietary patterns, hydration habits, and healthy sleep hygiene.
   - Emphasizing consistency, stress management, and daily recovery routines.

2. **Strictly Prohibited Medical Claims**:
   - **Diagnostic Claims**: Under no circumstances should the system diagnose or imply diagnosis of medical conditions (e.g., "You have diabetes", "This confirms cardiovascular disease", "Your symptoms prove asthma").
   - **Medication & Treatment Prescriptions**: Never recommend, prescribe, or advise changes to pharmaceutical drugs, clinical dosages, or medical treatments (e.g., "Take ibuprofen 400mg", "Stop taking your blood pressure medication").
   - **Unsupported Disease-Curing Claims**: Never claim a wellness habit or food item cures, treats, or reverses acute or chronic disease.

3. **Red-Flag Symptoms and Mandatory Escalation**:
   - If user reports symptoms such as:
     - Acute, crushing, or radiating chest pain / pressure
     - Unexplained shortness of breath or sudden respiratory distress
     - Sudden numbness, speech difficulty, facial drooping, or dizziness
     - Severe sudden headache, fainting, or acute abdominal pain
   - The system MUST refuse wellness recommendations and display immediate escalation:
     *"Your input may require professional medical evaluation. Please consult a qualified healthcare professional immediately."*
