# Sleep Hygiene and Restorative Recovery Guidance

## Overview
Quality sleep is a foundational pillar of preventive health, immune function, metabolic regulation, cognitive performance, and cardiovascular recovery.

## Core Sleep Recommendations
1. **Sleep Duration Target**:
   - Healthy adults require 7 to 9 hours of restorative sleep per night. Chronically obtaining less than 6 hours correlates with elevated resting heart rate, impaired glucose metabolism, and increased systemic stress.

2. **Circadian Rhythm Consistency**:
   - Maintain a consistent sleep-wake schedule 7 days a week, going to bed and waking up within a 30-minute window, even on weekends.
   - Seek natural morning sunlight exposure for 15 to 30 minutes within an hour of waking to anchor the circadian clock and optimize nighttime melatonin onset.

3. **Optimal Sleep Environment**:
   - Keep the bedroom quiet, dark, and cool (approximately 65°F to 68°F / 18°C to 20°C).
   - Dim artificial lighting and limit blue-light screen exposure (phones, tablets, laptops) for at least 45 to 60 minutes before bedtime.

4. **Evening Wind-Down Routines**:
   - Establish a 20 to 30-minute relaxing bedtime ritual (light reading, deep breathing exercises, gentle stretching, or warm shower).
   - Avoid caffeine intake within 6 to 8 hours of bedtime and avoid heavy or spicy meals within 2 to 3 hours of sleep.
