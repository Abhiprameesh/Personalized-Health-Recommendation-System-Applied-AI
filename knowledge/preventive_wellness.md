# Preventive Lifestyle Routines and Stress Resilience

## Overview
Preventive wellness focuses on daily sustainable behavioral habits that reduce cardiovascular risk, enhance metabolic stability, support mental vitality, and prevent chronic illness onset.

## Core Preventive Habit Principles
1. **Hydration and Fluid Balance**:
   - Proper hydration supports kidney function, cellular transport, cognitive alert state, and joint lubrication.
   - Drink water consistently throughout the day (aiming for 8 to 10 glasses or roughly 2 to 2.5 liters for non-strenuous days). Increase electrolyte and fluid replacement during intensive physical exertion.

2. **Active Living and Micro-Movement Routines**:
   - Cumulative daily movement (aiming for 7,000 to 10,000 steps daily) improves insulin sensitivity and circulation.
   - Break prolonged sedentary blocks with 2 to 5 minutes of mobility exercises, desk stretches, or brief corridor walks every hour.

3. **Resting Heart Rate and Recovery Markers**:
   - A normal resting heart rate for adults ranges from 60 to 100 beats per minute, with well-conditioned individuals often exhibiting lower rates (50 to 70 bpm).
   - Sustained elevations in baseline resting heart rate accompanied by fatigue may indicate overtraining, insufficient sleep, dehydration, or psychological stress, signaling a need for recovery days and light restorative activity.

4. **Stress Reduction & Mind-Body Regulation**:
   - Dedicate 5 to 15 minutes daily to parasympathetic activation: slow diaphragmatic breathing (such as 4-7-8 breathing or box breathing), progressive muscle relaxation, or quiet mindfulness.
   - Regular engagement in outdoor nature walks or leisure hobbies helps reduce salivary cortisol levels and systemic blood pressure.
